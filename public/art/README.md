# City map artwork

`hotel-building-v1.png` is an original generated hotel illustration with transparency. Map tiles keep each chain’s existing colour. Players can switch between Buildings and Logos above the map; their choice is saved locally in their browser. The logo mode preserves the previous tile artwork.

`hong-kong-japan-night-v1.png` is an original illustrated harbour panorama, combining a Hong Kong skyline and ferry with a Japanese hotel district. It appears as a shallow decorative strip above the game map; players can hide it, and the choice is saved locally. It depicts an imagined combined setting, not a real geographical view.

`hotel-entrances-night-v2.png` replaces the active harbour panorama with a hotel-focused illustration: grand entrances, canopies, warm lobbies and a Japanese hotel frontage. The previous panorama is retained for rollback. The shallow banner and local hide/show preference remain.

`peninsula-okura-night-v3.png` is the active poster: a stylised interpretation of The Peninsula Hong Kong (left) and The Okura Tokyo (right). The game overlays exact English lettering on the red/blue signboards for readability. The separate corner name boxes have been removed; the illustrated hotel signs remain. These accents match their chain colours. Previous art remains for rollback. Architectural references: https://www.peninsula.com/en/hong-kong/5-star-luxury-hotel-kowloon and https://theokuratokyo.jp/en/ . This is illustrative, not a photograph or exact reconstruction.
